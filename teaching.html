Teaching

I have taught theoretical physics and astrophysics at the University of Oxford, including a lecture course on magnetohydrodynamics. I make the course materials freely available for students and anyone interested in the fluid dynamics of conducting plasmas.

Oxford lecture course

Magnetohydrodynamics

A ten-lecture introduction to magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), the framework used to describe electrically conducting fluids threaded by magnetic fields. MHD underpins much of plasma astrophysics, from accretion discs and jets to stars and the interstellar medium.

The repository contains the complete compiled lecture notes as well as a separate PDF for each of the ten lectures.

RayTracer

A pedagogical code for computing photon geodesics in general relativity (Python / C++), written for students learning how light travels in curved spacetime. GitHub

It is technically way out of date and is not to be used for research. For production ray tracing, use the Kerr photon treatment in FitTeD.

Supervision and mentoring

I enjoy working with students on black-hole accretion, tidal disruption events, relativistic fluid dynamics, and the connection between analytic theory, simulations, and observations. Prospective students and early-career researchers are welcome to get in touch.
