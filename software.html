Software

Public tools for fitting transients and X-ray spectra with relativistic disc models live under the FitTeD organisation on GitHub. Many of the repositories currently on my personal account will be migrated there over the next few months.

FitTeD

Fitting Transients with Discs — a Python package for light curves and spectra of evolving relativistic accretion discs (Mummery, Nathan, Ingram & Gardner 2025, MNRAS 544, 2225). Used for multi-epoch TDE spectral fitting (including GSN 069 in pyXspec).

GitHub · docs · paper

TDEFLARE

Rapid black hole masses from TDE optical/UV flares, calibrated on thermal X-ray continua and late-time UV plateaus. Seconds on a laptop; does not require a detected plateau. 89 TDE masses in the accompanying paper. TDEFLARE is part of the FitTeD package.

GitHub · paper

tidalspin

Bayesian constraints on black hole spin from the relativistic tidal forces in a tidal disruption event, given a prior on the black hole mass (Mummery 2024, MNRAS 527, 6233). Uses only the Hills-mass limit; does not require a disc spectrum.

GitHub · paper

XSPEC / spectral models

  • fullkerr — continuum emission from within the ISCO.
  • TDEdiscXraySpectrum — TDE disc spectra in XSPEC.
  • superkerr — continuum fitting in the general Kerr metric (including super-extremal spins).
  • superkerrline — iron-line / reflection models in the full Kerr metric.
  • GR_prop_fluc — variability of relativistic accretion flows.
